THE LITTLE DETAILS

Good to know.

Questions before you make it yours? We’ve got you.

How do I place an order?+

Add your picks to the bag, sign in or create an account, enter your delivery details, and pay securely online. You can track the order from your account.

Can I pay on the website?+

Yes. Pay securely by card or mobile money through Paystack. Your order is marked paid only after Paystack confirms the transaction. Your order total includes any shipping charge.

Where do you deliver?+

We’ll confirm delivery arrangements with you after you place your order.

Are all items always in stock?+

We source in small batches, so availability can change. We’ll confirm each item with you personally.
