A NOTE FROM THE FRIENDS

We go looking.
You find your thing.

Trikonic started with three friends, a shared love of discovering the unexpected, and the belief that everyday things can feel a little more special.

We bring together useful, lovely, and sometimes delightfully unusual finds from different places. Each one is picked with care and shared in small batches.

THE LITTLE DETAILS

Good to know.

Questions before you make it yours? We’ve got you.

How do I place an order?+

Add your picks to the bag and send your order request. We’ll reach out to confirm availability, delivery details, and the total before anything is finalized.

Can I pay on the website?+

Online payment is coming soon. For now, submitting an order is a request only; we’ll contact you directly to agree on payment and delivery.

Where do you deliver?+

Delivery options and costs are confirmed with you after we receive your order request.

Are all items always in stock?+

We source in small batches, so availability can change. We’ll confirm each item with you personally.
